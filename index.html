Our Story

Where tradition meets a modern plate

Eko Bites started as one bubbling pot of jollof at a Lekki family gathering. Today we bring that same table to yours — dine-in, takeaway, or delivered hot across Lagos. Every recipe is grandmother-tested and Lagos-approved, no shortcuts, no substitutes.

From office lunch packs to full event catering, we cook in batches big enough for the whole compound and small enough to still taste homemade.
